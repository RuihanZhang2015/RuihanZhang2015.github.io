Course projects

Fall 2022

Multi-view 6D object pose refinement with differentiable rendering

6.S980 Machine Learning for Inverse Graphics · MIT EECS

Designed a loss for refining object-pose predictions by gradient descent that widens the basin of convergence 1.5-fold, combining single-view photometric, structural-similarity, and perceptual losses with object-level pose differences for bundle adjustment.

Spring 2022

Towards photo-realistic face-swapped videos

6.869 Computer Vision · MIT EECS

A face-swapping pipeline to protect the identities of Asian women interviewees: synthetic faces from StyleGAN, swapping with SimSwap, and video super-resolution to produce photo-realistic results.

Spring 2022

Solving put option prices with dynamic programming

6.231 Dynamic Programming and Reinforcement Learning · MIT EECS

Approximate value- and policy-iteration algorithms for the optimal stopping policy of an American put option, outperforming the vanilla algorithm; compared basis functions and multilayer perceptrons for value and Q-function approximation and generalized to multi-stock indexes.

Fall 2021

Affect encoding in word embeddings

6.864 Natural Language Processing · MIT EECS

How NLP models such as contextualized BERT encode affect-related meaning and relate to human judgment, using PCA, Spearman correlation, and logistic regression. A BERT model fine-tuned on affect-value regression outperformed vanilla models in transfer and few-shot settings.
